# Security policy

This policy applies to every Galvan SRL repository and to the software we ship —
including [Tomomi](https://tomomi.app) and Soki.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **[hello@galvan.dev](mailto:hello@galvan.dev)** with `SECURITY` in the
subject line. Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

Useful things to include, as far as you have them:

- What the issue is and roughly how severe you think it is
- Which product, version or URL is affected
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Anything you already know about how far it reaches

We read every message ourselves and will normally acknowledge yours within two
working days. We will tell you what we find, whether or not we agree it is a
vulnerability, and we will let you know when a fix ships.

## What we ask

Please give us a reasonable chance to fix an issue before describing it publicly.
While you are investigating, work against your own accounts and your own data —
don't access, modify or retain anyone else's, and don't run anything that degrades
service for other people.

## Scope

Our products, our infrastructure and the code in this organisation are in scope.
Third-party services we depend on are not; report those to the vendor. Findings
that are only theoretical, or that come from an automated scanner without a
demonstrated impact, are usually not actionable on their own.

## Recognition

We don't run a paid bug bounty. If you report something real and want to be
credited when the fix ships, say so and we will name you.
